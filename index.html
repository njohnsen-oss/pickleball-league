How ratings work

The switch above ranks by one of four numbers. The table on a computer shows all of them.

Rating

The league rating. Every game in the league's history is used at once to find the set of ratings that best explains who beat whom. Bigger wins count a little more than close ones. Everyone starts at 1000; the number has no ceiling.

New players show P (provisional) until they have 8 games and enough variety of partners and opponents for the rating to settle. The confidence bar shows how settled it is.

DUPR est.

An estimate on the scale USA Pickleball uses (DUPR, 2.0 to 8.0), worked out from league games only. Before each game it predicts the score from the two teams' average ratings: a half-point gap expects about 11–5. Your rating moves on points scored versus that prediction, so a close loss to a stronger team can raise it and a narrow win over a weaker team can lower it. Recent games count a bit more, and players with only a few games stay near the middle until they've played more.

This is not an official DUPR. Official ratings come from matches recorded with DUPR. If a league player has a real DUPR, an admin can enter it in Settings and every estimate lines up to it.

Win %

Games won out of games played. Easy to read, but it doesn't care who you played or who your partner was.

+/− (point differential)

Your team's points minus the other team's, averaged per game. A close loss barely hurts and a blowout counts for a lot.

Doubles

In every method a doubles team counts as the average of its two players, and both partners get the team's result and point margin.

Fixing a bad result

Edit or delete the game on History, then use Settings → Recalculate ratings. Every number here is rebuilt from the full game history, so nothing is lost.
