How team ratings work

Team rating is how strong a pair is together, on the same scale as the player rating. It’s worked out from their results against the teams they actually faced (each opposing pair counts as the average of its two ratings), with bigger wins counting a little more.

A new pair leans on the average of the two players’ own ratings until they’ve played a few games together. Under 8 games together shows a P and ranks below the rest.

Every doubles game the two played on the same side counts, including games from before the team was made. Removing a team keeps all its games.
